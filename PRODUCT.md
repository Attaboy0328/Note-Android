# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Mobile-first writers and note-takers who capture fiction material, fleeting ideas, links, and short reflections throughout the day. They are usually working from a phone, often with one hand, and need a calm place to save something quickly without being pulled into a heavy workspace.

## Product Purpose
Note is a lightweight, local-first PWA for quick writing and retrieval. Its job is to make capture feel immediate, reading feel quiet, and organization feel low-friction. Success looks like opening fast, writing instantly, staying readable over long sessions, and keeping data dependable through offline storage with optional cloud sync.

## Brand Personality
Calm, attentive, dependable.

The interface should feel paper-like and considered, but still modern enough to behave like a native mobile tool. It should support focus rather than perform personality too loudly.

## Anti-references
Avoid flashy productivity SaaS patterns, neon dashboards, overly gamified task-app aesthetics, and social-feed card UIs. Avoid decorative complexity that competes with writing, especially loud gradients, oversized badges, or dense menu chrome that makes the app feel like a control panel instead of a notebook.

## Design Principles
1. Capture should feel instant. The path from opening the app to writing must stay obvious and fast.
2. Writing comes before chrome. Interface elements should recede when the user is reading, scanning, or editing notes.
3. Mobile comfort is a product feature. Touch targets, spacing, and gesture thresholds should support one-handed use.
4. Calm hierarchy beats spectacle. The app should signal structure through spacing, rhythm, and type, not through loud decoration.
5. Local-first trust matters. The product should always communicate that notes are safe, readable, and available even when sync is optional or delayed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast across notes, controls, and metadata. Keep touch targets comfortably tappable on mobile, respect reduced-motion expectations, preserve clear focus states, and avoid interactions that rely only on precise gestures. Since the shell is app-like and zoom is disabled, text sizing and spacing should stay readable by default on common phone viewports.
